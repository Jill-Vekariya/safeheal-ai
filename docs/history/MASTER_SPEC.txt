**Can an evidence-grounded LLM agent improve the accuracy of incident diagnosis and the effectiveness of safe recovery in distributed systems compared with deterministic rule-based approaches?**

Implement and evaluate three approaches:

**Baseline A — Native Kubernetes Recovery**
- Use ordinary Kubernetes restart, reconciliation, and health-check mechanisms.
- Measure what Kubernetes can and cannot recover automatically.

**Baseline B — Rule-Based Incident Controller**
- Use metrics and predefined rules to identify incidents.
- Recommend or perform explicitly permitted recovery actions.

**Proposed Approach C — AI-Assisted Diagnosis and Recovery**
- Analyze relevant telemetry.
- Generate structured root-cause hypotheses.
- Provide evidence for diagnostic conclusions.
- Propose actions selected from predefined runbooks.
- Pass all actions through an independent safety policy controller.
- Evaluate whether AI adds measurable value.

Use comparable failure scenarios, measurement definitions, and trial procedures.
