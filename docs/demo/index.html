    This prototype illustrates the SafeHeal AI workflow. Values, diagnoses, events, and time offsets are scripted. It does not run a model, contact a cluster, or reproduce experimental results.
    1. Observe the incident.Run one of six synthetic failure scenarios.
    2. Inspect the boundary.Review evidence and a diagnosis with execution_allowed: false.
    3. Preview the action.Independent policy leads to a dry run or a denial.
    4. Approve a simulation.For eligible cases, illustrate one write and separate verification.
    The recovery contract was checked against main revision 97c9aede on 29 October 2026. This demo simplifies the Python controller and does not establish production safety, AI accuracy, or an AI performance advantage.
